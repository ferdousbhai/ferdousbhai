# Ferdous Bhai's kitchen

## What I'm cooking

### Omarchy [All apps and install commands](/omarchy)

- [Recall](https://ferdousbhai.com/recall): Local semantic search for files, photos, recordings and code, with a CLI and bundled skill for agents.
- [Ghost](https://ferdousbhai.com/ghost): An orchestration layer for all your agent harnesses on Omarchy.
- [iCloud for Omarchy](https://ferdousbhai.com/icloud): Apple Notes, Photos and Find My as native apps, on one iCloud sign-in.
- [Omacloud](https://omacloud.computer): Your files and settings on every Omarchy computer, end-to-end encrypted and kept by other Omarchy computers.
- [Cloudmail](https://ferdousbhai.com/cloudmail): Email for people and organizations on their own domains, plus Gmail, iCloud Mail and HEY.

### Web apps

- [CloudChef](https://cloudchef.build): A Cloudflare-native app builder.
- [spicytrade](https://spicy.trade): An options-trading web app and MCP server, with tastytrade support.
- [Purple](https://soundspurple.com): Make music with your AI agent in a Strudel live coding studio, connected through MCP.

### Chrome extensions

- [Slop Detector](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/slop-detector/ifigliidkcoojgohcbmmaonifoppnmoh): Detects AI-flavoured writing.

### Agent tools

- [keep-going](https://github.com/ferdousbhai/keep-going): Hooks and plugins that keep AI agents working until the job is done.
