# Ferdous' software projects

## Software projects

### Omarchy

- [Recall for Omarchy](https://ferdousbhai.com/recall): Local semantic search for files, photos, recordings and code, with a CLI and bundled skill for agents.
- [Ghost](https://ferdousbhai.com/ghost): An orchestration layer for all your agent harnesses on Omarchy.
- [iCloud for Omarchy](https://ferdousbhai.com/icloud): Apple Notes, Photos, Find My, Reminders and Contacts as native apps.
- [Omacloud](https://github.com/ferdousbhai/omacloud): Your files and settings synced across Omarchy computers, end-to-end encrypted.
- [Cloudmail](https://ferdousbhai.com/cloudmail): Email on your own domains, run from your own Cloudflare account, with a Screener for new senders. Gmail and HEY too.

### Web apps

- [CloudChef](https://cloudchef.build): A Cloudflare-native app builder.
- [spicytrade](https://spicy.trade): An options-trading web app and MCP server, with tastytrade support.
- [Purple](https://soundspurple.com): A Strudel music studio your agent plays over MCP.

### Chrome extensions

- [Slop Detector](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/slop-detector/ifigliidkcoojgohcbmmaonifoppnmoh): Detects AI-flavoured writing.

### Agent tools

- [keep-going](https://github.com/ferdousbhai/keep-going): Hooks and plugins that keep AI agents working until the job is done.
